CISTERN v3.0.0-dev — sanitation protocol for Sector 7

THE CONCEPT
  Route need to capacity.  Convert waste to income.
  Contamination is the clock.

GLYPHS
  ▓ wall    · floor    ◆ ore vein    ─ pipe    Ω toilet
  ▣ tank    ▒ contamination    + gate    α..θ workers

THE LOOP
  Workers mine ◆ for alloy.  Their bladders fill.  At 60%%
  they walk to a Ω and seat themselves — entering the toilet
  tile IS sitting down.  A Ω works only when piped to a ▣ with
  headroom.  Each use sends 10 units down the line.

  A full ▣ backs up every Ω it feeds (red Ω).  Purge with x on
  the ▣: free, and it PAYS 1 alloy per 3 units of waste.

  At 100%% a worker breaches: the tile turns ▒, contamination
  rises, neighbors fall sick.  ▒ spreads to adjacent floor.
  Decon with c.  At 20 the sector is condemned.

  Every 40 ticks a migrant arrives.  Population means load.

CONTROLS
  SPC / RET   advance one tick
  arrows / mouse   move cursor
  t   build toilet (10)     p   lay pipe (2)
  K   build tank (15)       c   decontaminate (3)
  x   purge tank (pays)     d   demolish (3)
  r   auto-run (5 ticks/s)
  T   skip tutorial         n   new game
  ?   this briefing         q   quit
